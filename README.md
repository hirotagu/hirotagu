# Hirokazu Taguchi, MD

## About Me

I am an internist specializing in rheumatology and connective tissue diseases. I am currently pursuing a PhD at Kyoto University Graduate School of Medicine, where my research focuses on immune-related adverse events (irAEs) associated with immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) in cancer treatment.

## Research Interests

- Immune-related adverse events (irAEs)
- Metabolomics
- Multi-omics
- Machine learning
- Clinical immunology

## Current Research

### Multi-Omics Research

My research focuses on identifying biomarkers to better understand the pathophysiology of irAEs and predict their development using metabolomics-centered multi-omics data obtained from human clinical samples.

I am particularly interested in predicting the risk of irAEs before ICI initiation, with the long-term goal of translating predictive models into clinical practice.

### Clinical Research

I conduct clinical research on irAEs using an institutional database of more than 2,000 patients treated with ICIs. I am also involved in establishing multicenter collaborative studies in Japan.
